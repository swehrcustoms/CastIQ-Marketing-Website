# Subscription approval decisions

These are unresolved. They were not invented as product rules.

1. Freemium full-report cap (the pricing table says “Proposed: 3 / 30 days” and states the limit is off).
2. Saved-lake caps (proposed 2 / 10 / unlimited). They are not enforced. Favorites already work for signed-in anglers.
3. What “extended history”, “advanced filters”, and “historical trends” include, and when they are actually available.
4. Whether new-report alerts may be sold before email dispatch exists (`VITE_PREMIUM_EMAIL_DISPATCH_ENABLED` is off).
5. Grace period after a failed payment. None is defined. Missing billing data stays on the free tier.
6. A binding refund policy. `/legal/refunds` says billing is not live.
7. A free trial. Not approved. Not advertised.
8. Permission to set `VITE_PAID_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=true` or `LIVE_CHECKOUT_ENABLED`.

Until those are approved, paid buttons must not charge and enforcement must stay off.
